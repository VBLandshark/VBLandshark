# VBLandshark's Rudee Inlet Surf and Fish Conditions

A single-page dashboard for Rudee Inlet and the Virginia Beach oceanfront: weather, tides, waves, offshore buoys, radar, solunar fishing times, best times to fish and surf, and oceanfront food and drink specials.

`index.html` is the whole site. Live data loads in the browser from NOAA, the National Weather Service, CDIP (Scripps) and Open-Meteo. The Food & Drink tab is updated weekly.
